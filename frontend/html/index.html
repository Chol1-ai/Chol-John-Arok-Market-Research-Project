Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Choose a restaurant, add meals to your cart, and continue to checkout. For delivery, select an available area and enter a delivery landmark.

Can I order from either restaurant?

Yes. Choose Foodizo at Ham Towers or Obunuzi opposite YMCA. Menus and availability are shown for the selected outlet.

How do I pay?

Pickup orders are paid for at the outlet counter. Delivery orders are paid for in cash on delivery.

How do I check my order status?

Use the order token shown after placing your order to open its tracker. The tracker refreshes the status automatically.

Where do you deliver?

Delivery is available only in the areas listed for the selected outlet. Choose an area at checkout to see its fee.

How can I ask about or change an order?

Contact the restaurant you selected as soon as possible. Changes may not be possible once preparation or delivery has started.

About Foodizo & Obunuzi

Two Wandegeya restaurant locations

Foodizo at Ham Towers and Obunuzi Restaurant & Cafe opposite YMCA serve customers in Wandegeya, Kampala. Browse each outlet’s menu and choose pickup or delivery in an available area.

Terms & Conditions

Please review these ordering terms before submitting an order:

  • Check the selected outlet, items, quantities, fulfillment method, and displayed total before placing your order.
  • Menu items and availability may differ between outlets and can change.
  • Pickup orders are paid for at the selected outlet’s counter. Delivery orders are paid for in cash on delivery; delivery is limited to the listed areas and fees.
  • Keep your order token available to check status. Contact the selected restaurant promptly if you need to ask about a submitted order; changes depend on its preparation or delivery status.
  • Provide accurate contact and delivery details so the restaurant can fulfill your order.

Privacy Policy

Information used for your order

When you place an order, the restaurant receives the details needed to fulfill it, such as your name, phone number, selected outlet, items, and fulfillment method. Delivery orders also include the selected delivery area and the landmark or address note you provide. Restaurant staff use these details to prepare, deliver, and help you track the order.

Saved in your browser

Your cart, selected outlet, active order token, and notification preferences may be saved in this browser so you can continue using the service. Clearing this browser’s site data removes those saved details, but does not cancel or delete an order already submitted to the restaurant.

Ready notifications

Ready notifications are optional. If enabled, your browser will ask for permission to show a notification when your order is ready. You can turn notifications off in your browser or device settings.

For questions about information connected with an order, contact the restaurant you selected.
